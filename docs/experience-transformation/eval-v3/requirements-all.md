# Independent travel-quality requirements: ten scenarios

These requirements were written from public sources without looking at any generated itinerary or at Sidequest code. They describe what a competent human travel advisor would expect a plan to contain.

**How to read this**
- Every claim cites a URL. Many official sites could not be fetched or did not appear in search results. Where a claim rests only on an operator or blog page, it is marked *(secondary)*. Where it rests on general advisor knowledge that was not checked against a source in this pass, it is marked *unverified*.
- **Sun times.** timeanddate.com refused automated fetches (HTTP 403), so sunrise and sunset were computed with the NOAA sunrise/sunset algorithm (zenith 90.833°). They should agree with a calculator to within about ±2 minutes, and you can check them at the timeanddate.com URL given for each place. The local offsets used are 2027 summer time where it applies: Lisbon UTC+1, Rome UTC+2, Iceland UTC+0, Bishkek UTC+6, Tanzania UTC+3, Japan UTC+9, Hawaii UTC−10. The NOAA method is described at https://gml.noaa.gov/grad/solcalc/calcdetails.html.
- "Daily limit" means time spent moving between places (driving, transfers, trains). It does not include walking that is itself the activity.

## 1. family-wellness-march: "family spring retreat, 19–29 March, feel good, outdoors" (no destination given)

**What the request means.** The traveller wants **restoration**: low stress, outdoor time, and a family that includes children. "Retreat" and "feel good because life has been stressful" outrank sightseeing coverage. The trip is 10 nights.

**Defining experiences (any good answer must have them)**
- **Restful outdoor time every day**: easy walks, beaches, lakes, forests or gardens, at a gentle pace. *unverified (derived from the request)*
- **A restorative element**: hot springs, onsen, spa, sea swimming, or a nature lodge. *unverified*
- **Family-suitable activities**: short trails, beaches with lifeguards, gentle boat trips, wildlife. *unverified*
- **Unscheduled time**: whole half-days with nothing planned. *unverified*

**Recommended structure**
- **One base**, two at most, for 10 nights. Each hotel move adds packing, check-in and transfer stress, which works against the stated goal. *unverified (advisor norm)*
- At most one transfer day longer than about 2 hours, apart from arrival and departure.
- The weather should be mild in late March, roughly 15–25 °C, and the destination should not be in its rainy or monsoon season.

**Destination types that fit**
These are illustrations, not prescriptions:
- Subtropical Atlantic islands such as the Canary Islands or Madeira. *unverified*
- The southern Portuguese or Spanish coast, with spring hiking and mild weather. *unverified*
- Japanese onsen and countryside, where late March is cherry-blossom time in the south. The JMA average first bloom is 24 March in Tokyo and 26 March in Kyoto: https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e2011_when.html
- Hawaii. *unverified*
- Costa Rica in the dry season. *unverified*
- US desert national-park lodges such as Sedona or Zion, flagged for spring-break crowds. *unverified*
- Northern New Zealand in early autumn. *unverified*

**Destination types that contradict the intent**
- A multi-base road trip with long daily drives.
- High-altitude trekking.
- A dense city break.
- Destinations in their hot or rainy pre-monsoon season. Mainland Southeast Asia heats up sharply in March and April. *unverified*
- Cold or snowbound places pitched as "outdoors". *unverified*
- A safari built on many long transfer days.

**Hard requirements**
- Kid-appropriate effort levels: no strenuous all-day hikes.
- Every day has some outdoor time.
- No more than one hotel change.
- The plan states the late-March weather it expects.

**Red flags (fail the plan)**
- Starts at 05:00–06:00, for example sunrise hikes or early tour pickups. One optional exception is acceptable if it is clearly marked.
- More than two bases.
- Repeated days with 3 or more hours of transfers.
- Nightlife-led or packed museum days.
- High-altitude or technical hikes.
- Destinations in hurricane, monsoon or extreme-heat season.
- Activities unsuitable for children, or that legally exclude them.
- No rest or free time anywhere in the plan.

**Timing facts**
- The dates usually overlap Easter and spring-break crowds. Easter Sunday 2027 is 28 March. *unverified, from the ecclesiastical calendar*
- Sun times depend on the destination chosen.

**Reasonable daily limits**
- About 2 hours of transfer per day at most; typically under 1 hour.
- Walks of no more than about 8 km for children. *unverified*

## 2. kumano-kodo: Kumano Kodo, Japan, 19–29 March 2027, family of 2 adults and 2 children

**Defining experiences**
- **Walking the Nakahechi pilgrimage route**: the main route linking the three Grand Shrines of Kumano. Its sections run from Takijiri-oji to Tsugizakura-oji, then from Tsugizakura-oji to Kumano Hongu Taisha. https://www.tb-kumano.jp/en/kumano-kodo/nakahechi/
- **Kumano Hongu Taisha**, plus Oyunohara (the original shrine site, *unverified*), arrived at on foot through Hosshinmon-oji. Hosshinmon-oji to Hongu is about 7 km and 2–3 hours, a family-friendly stretch. *(secondary, the visitwakayama/tb-kumano figures quoted in search results)* https://www.tb-kumano.jp/en/kumano-kodo/nakahechi/
- **The other two Grand Shrines**: Kumano Nachi Taisha with Nachi Falls, and Kumano Hayatama Taisha. https://www.tb-kumano.jp/en/kumano-kodo/nakahechi/ (the "three Grand Shrines" framing)
- **Overnight stays in an onsen or village minshuku**: Yunomine, Kawayu or Watarase onsen near Hongu, or Chikatsuyu village. Accommodation sits in isolated villages served by local buses and is booked through KUMANO TRAVEL. https://www.tb-kumano.jp/en/kumano-kodo/nakahechi/ (onsen names *unverified*)

**Recommended structure**
- Gateway: Kii-Tanabe, reached by JR limited express from Osaka/Kyoto. *unverified*
- A classic walk is Takijiri-oji to Hongu, about 38 km over 2–3 walking days:
  - Takijiri-oji to Chikatsuyu is about 13 km. https://visitwakayama.jp/en/itineraries/detail_147.html *(search excerpt)*
  - Chikatsuyu to Hongu is about 24 km and roughly 8 hours, which is usually split or shortened by bus for families. *(secondary)*
- After the walk, Nachi and Shingu by bus.
- Before or after, Kyoto or Osaka (10 nights total gives room for both).
- Bases: Tanabe (1 night), Chikatsuyu or Takahara (1), Hongu area onsen (2), then Nachi/Katsuura or Shingu (1), with city nights at either end.
- Movement is **on foot**, with **luggage shuttles** between inns. A Nakahechi luggage shuttle exists and can be booked through KUMANO TRAVEL: https://www.tb-kumano.jp/en/kumano-kodo/nakahechi/. Local buses fill the gaps. No car is needed.

**Hard requirements**
- The trip must include **real walking on the Kodo**, at least one full section and ideally 2–3 days.
- Stages must be child-appropriate: about 7–13 km per day, with bus shortcuts.
- Village accommodation must be booked in advance; it is scarce. https://www.tb-kumano.jp/en/kumano-kodo/nakahechi/
- Luggage should be moved by shuttle, not carried.
- Walking should finish before dark, which falls around 18:07–18:15.

**Red flags**
- A Kodo trip with no walking, or only shrine visits by car or bus.
- Walking stages over about 15 km per day for children. The 24 km Chikatsuyu–Hongu stage done in one go is a flag.
- Walking that ends after sunset on mountain trail.
- A self-drive rental car as the main mode.
- No Hongu Taisha.
- Kawayu Senninburo (the river-dug onsen) presented as available. It is a winter-only installation that normally ends in February. *unverified*
- Families carrying full luggage between inns.

**Timing facts (19–29 March 2027)**
- **Kumano Hongu sun times** (computed; check at https://www.timeanddate.com/sun/japan/tanabe):

  | Date | Sunrise | Sunset |
  |---|---|---|
  | 19 Mar | 06:03 | 18:07 |
  | 24 Mar | 05:56 | 18:11 |
  | 29 Mar | 05:50 | 18:15 |

- **Cherry blossom.** The JMA average first bloom is 26 March in Kyoto and 24 March in Tokyo, with full bloom on 4 April and 31 March: https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e2011_when.html. Expect blossom to be starting in the Kansai cities at the end of the trip; Kii-peninsula timing is similar or slightly earlier. *unverified*
- **Holidays.** Vernal Equinox Day falls on about 21 March 2027, and Japanese school spring holidays run from late March. Expect more domestic travellers. *unverified*
- **Weather.** Cool, about 10–17 °C by day; rain is possible and trails get muddy. *unverified*

**Reasonable daily limits**
- Walking: about 7–13 km and 3–6 hours a day with children.
- Transfers: about 2 hours at most. Osaka to Tanabe by limited express is roughly 2 hours. *unverified*

## 3. iceland-ring-road: Iceland Ring Road, 14–22 June 2027, couple

**Defining experiences**
- **Driving the full Route 1 loop**: about 1,322 km and paved throughout. https://stuckiniceland.com/iceland-ring-road-plan *(secondary)*. The road is maintained by Vegagerðin.
- **South coast waterfalls and beach**: Seljalandsfoss, Skógafoss, Reynisfjara black-sand beach and Vík. https://guidetoiceland.is/travel-iceland/drive/south-iceland *(secondary)*
- **Jökulsárlón glacier lagoon and Diamond Beach**: directly on the Ring Road at the edge of Vatnajökull National Park. https://reykjavikattractions.is/attraction/the-ring-road/ *(secondary)*. Skaftafell sits nearby.
- **The north**: Mývatn, Dettifoss, Goðafoss and Akureyri; optionally whale watching from Húsavík. *unverified*
- **The Golden Circle**: Þingvellir, Geysir and Gullfoss. *unverified*
- **The East Fjords** as a scenic transit day. *unverified*
- **Puffins in season**: they nest from about May to August. https://visiticeland.com/article/summer-activities *(secondary)*
- **Midnight-sun light**: around the solstice Reykjavík has almost 24 hours of daylight. https://www.onthegotours.com/Iceland/Guides/midnight-sun *(secondary)*

**Recommended structure**
- 8 nights and 9 days for the full loop. Common advice is 7–10 days or more, and 8 nights is at the tight end. https://adventures.is/blog/iceland-ring-road-itinerary/ *(secondary)*
- 5–7 bases, moving most nights. A typical loop runs in either direction through Reykjavík, the South Coast (Vík), the Jökulsárlón/Höfn area, the East Fjords (Egilsstaðir), Mývatn/Akureyri, and back via the west or Borgarnes.
- Movement is a **self-drive rental car**. A 2WD car is fine for Route 1.

**Hard requirements**
- Complete the loop, or state the trade-off clearly if it is shortened.
- Include Jökulsárlón.
- Do not plan F-roads in a 2WD car.
- Highland F-roads open between about mid-June and early July, varying each year. The official averages are F206 Lakagígar about 20 June, F225 Landmannaleið about 16 June, and F261 about 5 July. https://www.vegagerdin.is/en/the-transportation-system/the-road-system/roads/opening-of-mountain-roads
- Respect Reynisfjara's sneaker-wave danger. https://www.laidbacktrip.com/posts/best-places-to-see-puffins-iceland *(secondary)*. See also https://safetravel.is *(not fetched)*.

**Red flags**
- Skipping Jökulsárlón.
- **Northern lights, aurora or stargazing** in June. There is no real darkness.
- "Sunset dinner at 20:00" style timing that ignores sunset at about 23:58–00:04.
- Repeated days of more than about 5 hours' driving.
- Highland or F-road plans (Landmannalaugar, Kerlingarfjöll, Askja) in a 2WD car, or before the roads open.
- Swimming at Reynisfjara.
- Dyrhólaey cliffs presented as open before about 23 June. Its nesting closure is reported as 15 May to 23 June. *(secondary)* https://www.laidbacktrip.com/posts/best-places-to-see-puffins-iceland
- A loop that is impossible in 8 nights, for example adding the Westfjords as well.

**Timing facts (14–22 June 2027)**
- **Sun times** (computed; check at https://www.timeanddate.com/sun/iceland/reykjavik):

  | Place and date | Sunrise | Sunset |
  |---|---|---|
  | Reykjavík, 14 Jun | 02:58 | 23:58 |
  | Reykjavík, 18 Jun | 02:55 | 00:03 (next day) |
  | Reykjavík, 22 Jun | 02:55 | 00:04 (next day) |
  | Höfn, 18 Jun | 02:26 | 23:38 |
  | Akureyri, 18 Jun | 01:34 | 00:54 (next day) |

  Civil twilight lasts all night.
- **Puffins** are at peak season. https://visiticeland.com/article/summer-activities *(secondary)*
- **Crowds.** High season begins. Iceland's National Day is 17 June. *unverified*
- **Weather.** About 10–15 °C, windy, with rain possible. *unverified*

**Reasonable daily limits**
- About 4–5 hours of driving a day at most, roughly 250–350 km. The rural speed limit is 90 km/h on paved roads (*unverified*), and stops are frequent.
- Leave enough slack that one bad-weather day does not break the loop.

## 4. kyrgyzstan: Kyrgyzstan adventure, 10–20 July 2027, friends

**Defining experiences**
- **A night in a Song-Kul yurt camp** with horse riding. Camps run from about June to mid-September, and July and August are best. https://kyrgyzstan-tourism.com/?p=8736 *(secondary operator)*. A 4WD is needed and there is no public transport. https://horizonguides.com/central-asia/song-kul-nomads-yurts-horses *(secondary)*
- **A Tian Shan trek from Karakol**, classically **Ala-Kul lake over the 3,860 m Ala-Kul pass to the Altyn-Arashan hot springs** over 2–4 days. The season is mid-June to mid-September. https://kyrgyzstan-tourism.com/?p=6104 *(secondary)*
- **Issyk-Kul lake**: the south shore, Skazka (Fairy Tale) canyon, and the Jeti-Oguz red rocks. *unverified*
- **Ala Archa National Park** near Bishkek as an acclimatisation day hike. *unverified*
- **Kochkor or community-based tourism (CBT) homestays**, and Burana Tower. *unverified*
- **Trekking information** from the national tourism portal: https://visitkyrgyzstan.org/experiences

**Recommended structure**
- 10 nights.
- Bases: Bishkek (1–2 nights) → Karakol (2 nights, plus 2–3 nights tented or in yurts on the trek) → south shore of Issyk-Kul (1) → Song-Kul (1–2, yurt) → Bishkek (1).
- Movement is a **hired car or 4WD with a driver**, or shared taxis and marshrutkas on the main roads; there is no rail. The last stretch to Song-Kul needs a 4WD. https://horizonguides.com/central-asia/song-kul-nomads-yurts-horses
- Bishkek to Karakol is about 405 km, roughly 7 hours. https://beyondwildplaces.com/how-to-get-from-bishkek-to-karakol-issyk-kul/ *(secondary)*
- Kochkor to Song-Kul is about 90 km, 3–4 hours by 4WD (sources disagree). https://horizonguides.com/central-asia/song-kul-nomads-yurts-horses *(secondary)*

**Hard requirements**
- Book a 4WD and driver for Song-Kul.
- Acclimatise before the 3,860 m pass by spending a day or more above 2,000 m first. *unverified (standard altitude practice)*
- Use a guide or a well-equipped self-guided setup. Yurt camps on the trail fill up in season. https://kyrgyzstan-tourism.com/?p=6104 *(secondary)*
- Plan for 4WD dirt roads that are weather-dependent over passes above 3,000 m.

**Red flags**
- Reaching Song-Kul by public bus or in a 2WD.
- Doing Bishkek → Karakol and Karakol → Song-Kul on consecutive days with no rest.
- Any single driving day of more than about 8 hours.
- Crossing the Ala-Kul pass on the day after arrival.
- An "adventure" trip with no trek or multi-day hike.
- No yurt night.
- A self-drive rental car on 4WD-only tracks without a driver.
- Promising snow-free passes or exact durations.

**Timing facts (10–20 July 2027)**
- **Sun times** (computed; check at https://www.timeanddate.com/sun/kyrgyzstan/karakol):

  | Place and date | Sunrise | Sunset |
  |---|---|---|
  | Karakol, 10 Jul | 05:18 | 20:25 |
  | Karakol, 15 Jul | 05:22 | 20:22 |
  | Karakol, 20 Jul | 05:26 | 20:19 |
  | Song-Kul, 15 Jul | 05:37 | 20:33 |

- **Season.** Peak trekking and yurt season; camps are open. *(secondary sources above)*
- **Weather.** Song-Kul sits at about 3,000 m: nights are near freezing and afternoon storms are common. *unverified*
- **Local events.** Mid-July hosts nomad horse games and festivals around Issyk-Kul and Song-Kul in some years. *unverified*

**Reasonable daily limits**
- Driving: about 6–7 hours a day at most, and only on transfer days.
- Trek stages: about 10–15 km and 800–1,200 m of ascent a day. *unverified*

## 5. tanzania-safari: Tanzania safari, 5–13 July 2027, couple

**Defining experiences**
- **The Serengeti during the Great Migration.** In early July the herds are moving from the Grumeti/Western Corridor north. From about mid-July, Mara River crossings start in the northern Serengeti around Kogatende and the Lamai Wedge. https://www.discoverafrica.com/blog/where-is-the-great-migration-in-july *(secondary)*, https://yellowzebrasafaris.com/us/tanzania/things-to-do/the-great-migration/ *(secondary)*
- **A Ngorongoro Crater game drive**. Gates open 06:00 and close 18:00. The Seneto descent gate closes at 16:00; Seneto is for descent only and Lerai for ascent only. https://www.ngorongorocrater.org/regulations.html
- **Tarangire**, with dry-season elephant concentrations, and/or Lake Manyara. *unverified*
- **Arusha or Kilimanjaro (JRO)** as the gateway. https://tanzania-specialist.com/travel-times-and-distances/tanzania/ *(secondary)*

**Recommended structure**
- 8 nights.
- Bases: Arusha (1 night on arrival), Tarangire (1–2), Ngorongoro highlands or Karatu (1–2), Serengeti (3–4, split central and north for the migration), with a flight back from a Serengeti airstrip.
- Movement is an **operator-guided 4x4 safari vehicle with a professional guide**, and/or light-aircraft fly-in between airstrips. Arusha to the Serengeti is about 1.5 hours by air versus a full day by road. https://www.anywhere.com/tanzania/questions/getting-around/driving-times *(secondary)*
- Indicative drive times *(secondary)*:
  - Arusha to Tarangire: about 2 hours.
  - Tarangire to Ngorongoro: about 2.5 hours.
  - Ngorongoro to central Serengeti: 3.5–5 hours.
  - Source: https://tanzania-specialist.com/travel-times-and-distances/tanzania/

**Hard requirements**
- A guided safari vehicle or fly-in, not self-drive. This is a red flag per the brief and standard practice.
- Crater descent timed so it starts before 16:00 at Seneto, with the vehicle out by gate close. https://www.ngorongorocrater.org/regulations.html
- Park entry and crater fees pre-arranged by the operator. *unverified*
- Malaria precautions. A yellow-fever certificate if arriving from an endemic country. *unverified*

**Red flags**
- **A self-drive rental car.**
- Walking or unguided activity inside the national parks.
- Guaranteed Mara River crossings in early July (they begin around mid-July and are never guaranteed).
- Crater floor visits beyond about 6 hours. A 6-hour per-vehicle cap is widely reported but not on the official rules page. *unverified*
- Arusha to the northern Serengeti by road in one day.
- No Serengeti.
- Night game drives inside the national parks, which are generally not permitted. *unverified*
- Beach or Zanzibar days that consume the safari in a 9-day trip. Optional is fine; replacing the safari is not.

**Timing facts (5–13 July 2027)**
- **Seronera sun times** (computed; check at https://www.timeanddate.com/sun/tanzania/arusha):

  | Date | Sunrise | Sunset |
  |---|---|---|
  | 5 Jul | 06:46 | 18:45 |
  | 13 Jul | 06:47 | 18:46 |

  Near the equator these barely change.
- **Season.** Dry season and peak season. June–October is peak. https://www.anywhere.com/tanzania/questions/getting-around/driving-times *(secondary)*
- **Highland nights.** Ngorongoro rim nights are cold, about 5–10 °C. *unverified*

**Reasonable daily limits**
- Point-to-point transfers: about 5–6 hours at most, game-driving en route.
- Game drives: 6–9 hours are normal.
- Prefer flights for Serengeti-north legs.

## 6. tokyo-kyoto-transit: Tokyo and Kyoto by train, 5–14 April 2027, couple

**Defining experiences**
- **Cherry blossom (hanami).** The JMA average full bloom is 31 March in Tokyo and 4 April in Kyoto. On these dates Tokyo will be past peak or falling, and Kyoto at peak or late. https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e2011_when.html
- **Tokyo**: Asakusa/Senso-ji, Meiji Jingu, Shibuya, Shinjuku Gyoen, Ueno. *unverified (canonical highlights)*
- **Kyoto**: Fushimi Inari, Kiyomizu-dera, Gion and Higashiyama, Arashiyama, the Philosopher's Path. *unverified*
- **The Shinkansen between them**: Nozomi takes about 2 h 10–20 min. https://www.japanesetrains.com/tokyo-to-kyoto *(secondary)*

**Recommended structure**
- 9 nights, 2 bases: Tokyo about 4–5 nights and Kyoto about 4 nights.
- Optional day trips by train: Nara from Kyoto (about 45 min, *unverified*), and Nikko, Kamakura or Hakone from Tokyo.
- Movement: **rail, subway and walking only.** A Japan Rail Pass holder pays a supplement of about ¥4,960 for Nozomi. https://www.jrpass.com/blog/booking-a-nozomi-train-ticket-with-your-japan-rail-pass *(secondary)*

**Hard requirements**
- **Train travel between cities.** No car or flight.
- Within cities, transit and walking.
- Blossom timing should be addressed honestly as late in Tokyo and peak or late in Kyoto.
- Major Kyoto sights should be visited early to avoid crowds. *unverified*

**Red flags**
- **A rental car or driving.**
- Flying Tokyo to Kyoto.
- "Golden Week" claims. Golden Week is late April to early May, outside the trip. *unverified*
- Promising full bloom in Tokyo on 10–14 April.
- Day trips that cost more than 3 hours of round-trip travel more than once.
- Repeated hotel changes within one city.
- A one-day Kyoto visit.
- Night-time temple visits when the temple is closed (most close at about 16:30–18:00, *unverified*), except special illuminations.

**Timing facts (5–14 April 2027)**
- **Sun times** (computed; check at https://www.timeanddate.com/sun/japan/tokyo and https://www.timeanddate.com/sun/japan/kyoto):

  | Place | Sunrise | Sunset |
  |---|---|---|
  | Tokyo, 5 Apr | 05:23 | 18:05 |
  | Tokyo, 14 Apr | 05:11 | 18:13 |
  | Kyoto, 5 Apr | 05:39 | 18:21 |
  | Kyoto, 14 Apr | 05:27 | 18:28 |

- **Crowds.** Peak sakura crowds and hotel prices. *unverified*
- **Weather.** About 12–20 °C. *unverified*

**Reasonable daily limits**
- About 90 minutes of transit within a city.
- One inter-city day of about 2.5–3 hours door to door.

## 7. city-break: Lisbon, Portugal, 13–16 May 2027, couple

**Red flags**

- Several day trips (Sintra plus Cascais plus Óbidos/Fátima) in a 4-day break.
- Promising the Santo António festivities, which are in June. *unverified*

**Timing facts (13–16 May 2027)**

- **Weather.** Mild and mostly dry, about 18–24 °C. *unverified*

## 8. beach-island: Kauai, Hawaii, 10–17 September 2027, couple

**Defining experiences**
- **The Nā Pali Coast**, seen by boat tour, helicopter, and/or the Kalalau Trail's first 2 miles to Hanakāpīʻai.
  - Hāʻena State Park (Kēʻē Beach and the Kalalau trailhead) **requires advance entry or parking reservations** for non-residents: $5 per person entry or $10 parking. https://dlnr.hawaii.gov/dsp/parks/kauai/haena-state-park/
  - The Kalalau Trail needs day-use or camping permits. https://dlnr.hawaii.gov/dsp/hiking/kauai/kalalau-trail/
- **Waimea Canyon and Kōkeʻe**: lookouts and rim trails. *unverified (official page not fetched)*
- **Hanalei Bay and the north shore; Poʻipū on the south shore.** *unverified*
- **Calm late-summer seas** suit Nā Pali boat tours, though tours may reroute in bad weather. https://kauaiseatours.com/best-time-to-visit-na-pali-coast-weather-ocean-conditions/ *(secondary operator)*

**Recommended structure**
- 7 nights. Bases: 1 (east side, Kapaʻa) or 2 (north shore Princeville/Hanalei plus south shore Poʻipū). *unverified (advisor norm)*
- Movement: a **rental car**. There is no road around Nā Pali, so the north and west ends do not connect. *unverified (well known)*

**Hard requirements**

## 12. point-to-point-trek: Tour du Mont Blanc trek, 10–19 July 2027, three friends, walking hut to hut / village to village

**What the request means.** Three friends want to **walk the Tour du Mont Blanc (TMB)**: a circuit around the Mont Blanc massif through France, Italy and Switzerland, moving every night between refuges, gîtes and village hotels. **Defining experiences**

  - Italy: Val Veny, Courmayeur, Val Ferret
